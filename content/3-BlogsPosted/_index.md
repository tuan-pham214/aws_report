During my internship, I wrote three articles to consolidate the AWS knowledge I had learned and share practical lessons with other beginners.

### [Blog 1 – Three Habits That Help AWS Beginners Avoid Unexpected Charges](3.1-Blog1/)

This article presents three simple habits: using a consistent Region, tagging resources when they are created, and checking and cleaning up resources after every lab.

### [Blog 2 – Reserved Concurrency vs. Provisioned Concurrency in AWS Lambda](3.2-Blog2/)

This article explains the different purposes of Reserved Concurrency and Provisioned Concurrency and how they can be combined to protect downstream systems while maintaining predictable latency.

### [Blog 3 – DynamoDB TTL Is Not a Timer](3.3-Blog3/)

This article explains why an expired DynamoDB item may remain visible for some time and why applications must enforce expiration in their own business logic.
