### 2. Main Content and Activities

FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week 2026 was a hackathon focused on Agents that perform multiple steps to solve practical problems. Through the online presentations and final session, I observed product demos and lessons from participating teams.

#### One Team’s KFC Voice AI Agent

One Team presented conversational food ordering through Zalo or WhatsApp. The Agent used product data and memory of customer preferences to complete an order without forcing the user to switch applications. The demo showed a response time of approximately three to five seconds and a low estimated cost per order.

#### Signal Scout’s Multi-Agent Strategic Analysis

Signal Scout collected competitor signals from financial reports, news, and shareholder communications. Multiple Agents connected the evidence and produced decision-support information with sources and confidence levels, allowing users to verify the analysis.

#### Team Plan’s Solutions Architect Assistant

Team Plan accepted natural-language requirements and generated Draw.io architecture diagrams, cost estimates, and Terraform or CloudFormation code. The output still required an engineer to review security, scalability, assumptions, and service limitations.

#### SHEPHERD Crowd Monitoring

Team 3K combined YOLO and ByteTrack to identify, track, and estimate crowd density at airports, supermarkets, and events. The system turned analysis into alerts and staff-allocation recommendations.

#### Six Pillar’s Adaptive Workflow Engine

Six Pillar addressed anti-money laundering investigations with specialized KYC, Money Flow, and Sanction Agents. The Agents prepared evidence, while a human retained responsibility for the final decision.

### 3. Outcomes and Value Gained

- Understood how Agentic AI divides work among Agents and tools before aggregating results.
- Learned practical applications in ordering, strategy analysis, architecture design, crowd monitoring, and financial compliance.
- Recognized the importance of starting from a user problem rather than from technology alone.
- Understood the roles of guardrails, evidence, and human-in-the-loop review.
- Learned to control scope and complete a stable MVP under time pressure.

A working core flow creates more value in a hackathon than many unfinished features.

### 4. Relevance to the Internship Project

The event reinforced the need to keep the Local AQI MVP focused on ingestion, forecasting, and alerts; define clear contracts among Backend, Data, and ML; validate forecast data before notifying users; prepare a short and stable demo; and keep `.env` files, keys, and credentials out of the repository.
