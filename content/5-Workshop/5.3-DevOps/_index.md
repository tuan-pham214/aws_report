This section covers AWS environment standardization, cost control, architecture and schedule coordination, system monitoring, integration testing, and final demo preparation for the **Local AQI Forecasting & Alert System** project.

It is organized into four sub-sections:

1. **Foundation & Governance**
2. **Project Coordination**
3. **Monitoring & Quality Assurance**
4. **Demo Preparation**

In particular:

- `1. Foundation & Governance` covers region, budget, service quotas, architecture, naming convention, and tag convention.
- `2. Project Coordination` covers task management, team dependencies, and evidence coordination.
- `3. Monitoring & Quality Assurance` covers logs, metrics, test cases, and end-to-end validation results.
- `4. Demo Preparation` covers the demo flow, speaking assignments, and evidence checks before recording.
